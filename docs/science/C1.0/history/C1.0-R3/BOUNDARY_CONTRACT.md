# Boundary contract — NUM-007/008, BC-001…BC-008

## BC-001 — meaning and applicability
SELECTED: equal-physical-area, frozen-composition reservoir half-Riemann coupling at the end of persistent T3. This is not the rejected algebraic Cd restriction. W2 and the measured physical contraction remain inside T3. No effective-area substitution, duplicated Cd, reinitialization or flux B at a physical boundary.

The local x axis points from reservoir into the pipe at either end; multiply stored axial velocity by n=+1 at the left end, −1 at the right end. Positive mass flux enters the pipe. A is the physical communicating area, Aopen in [0,Aface]. The remaining Aface−Aopen is a rigid wall, not removed storage. Reservoir inputs are its quiescent pR, TR, YR and provenance fractions; pR is both static and stagnation pressure in that reference, hR=h(TR,YR), sR=s(TR,pR,YR). Ambient uses measured static p, stagnation T at negligible ambient speed, dry-air chemical composition and X origin. A finite reservoir uses its current stage state, not a cached initial state. A two-zone reservoir uses the selected outgoing mixture Y/h from PHY-003; derive TR from that h at pR. Receiver energy receives actual donor total enthalpy, not h(TR).

At zero flux intensive donor values may be discontinuous; all advected extensive fluxes are exactly zero. Incoming exterior momentum/velocity is not a GEN1 input: a prescribed supersonic jet at this boundary is UNSUPPORTED_SUPERSONIC_INFLOW. Supersonic states *inside* T3 remain supported; its expansion supplies their acceleration.

## BC-003 — right wave curve from immediate interior state i
Use face-reconstructed Ui (first-order adjacent Ui if the joint admissibility limiter eliminates its slope). For each pressure p, define right-wave endpoint W(p)=(rhoD,vD,p,TD,Yi), connected to i by a 3-wave:

* Rarefaction p≤pi: solve s(TD,p,Yi)=si; vD=ui+integral(Ti→TD) cp(T,Yi)/a(T,Yi) dT. The fan speeds are vD+aD and ui+ai.
* Compression p>pi: solve h(TD)−hi−(p−pi)(1/rhoD+1/rhoi)/2=0, rhoD=p/(Ri TD), with TD>Ti and rhoD>rhoi. Set j=sqrt((p−pi)/(1/rhoi−1/rhoD)), vD=ui+(p−pi)/j and S=ui+j/rhoi. Require sD≥si within root error and vD+aD>S>ui+ai (equality only at zero strength). These are Rankine–Hugoniot/Lax checks, not a fitted switch.

At p=pi use i exactly. Rarefaction integrals are split at NASA interval breaks; adaptive Gauss–Kronrod 15/7, relative tolerance 1e−11 and absolute tolerance 1e−9 m/s. Entropy includes the ideal-mixture term; composition is fixed along this wave. Shock root may not be replaced by isentropic compression.

## BC-004 — solve and sample
If ui+ai≤0 and pR≤pi, the entire outgoing expansion is directed outside the domain: sample i immediately without constructing out-of-domain remote fan states. Otherwise first evaluate W(pR). If its endpoint velocity ≤0, the candidate is outflow: for a shock, sample i when S<0 and W(pR) when S>0. At S=0 use the conservative flux, which is identical on either side. For a fan, sample i if ui+ai≤0, W(pR) if vD+aD≥0, otherwise solve vD(p)+aD(p)=0 inside that fan and sample its sonic state. All advected composition and origin are Yi. This is sampling at x/t=0, not an imposed back pressure on a supersonic outflow.

If W(pR).vD>0, material enters. Construct reservoir isentrope pS(T)=pR exp((s°(T,YR)−s°(TR,YR))/RR), vS(T)=sqrt(2[hR−h(T,YR)]). Let Tcrit solve vS(T)²−a(T)²=0 on [300,TR], if it lies in the domain. The accessible subsonic curve runs from (TR,pR,0) to the first of the sonic state or domain endpoint. Solve f(T)=vS(T)−vD(pS(T))=0 on that interval. Then trace=(pS/(RR T),vS,pS,T,YR). The right-wave endpoint has the same p and v but Yi; the intervening contact carries the reservoir composition into the pipe. Require every shock speed, or minimum fan speed, to be ≥0 and the contact speed ≥0. If f(Tcrit)<0, the sampled reservoir trace is sonic: the reservoir cannot supply a subsonic endpoint compatible with the lower downstream pressure. This is the critical-capacity boundary; subsequent right-going waves are resolved inside T3. At f(Tcrit)=0 the two formulas coincide. If the interval ended at a domain limit rather than sonic, lack of a root is BOUNDARY_THERMOCHEMICAL_DOMAIN_VIOLATION, not permission to invent a sonic state. A root outside the allowed pressure range is also rejected.

Enumerate admissible roots on this subsonic reservoir segment; ordinary positive-cp convex GEN1 states give one. If multiple admissible roots are detected, fail INCOMPATIBLE_INCOMING_STATE; never select nearest/maximum-flow. The steady lossless inlet model is an explicitly selected reservoir approximation, not a uniqueness theorem for arbitrary restricted nozzles. T3 carries restriction history.

## BC-005 — root mechanics and failures
Pressure intervals are intersections of [50000,5000000] Pa with the isentropic or compression temperature-admissible interval. Construct endpoint temperature brackets using monotonic s(T,p) or the compression Hugoniot. Split at NASA transitions, and bracket pressure with 64 logarithmically spaced subintervals plus pi,pR and any sonic endpoint. Isolate every sign change; exact endpoint roots count once. A bracket is never extended outside the domain. Use safeguarded bisection (160 iterations maximum), with normalized residual ≤1e−10 AND relative bracket width ≤1e−10; temperature width ≤1e−8 K. Normalize velocities by max(ai,aR,1 m/s), pressure by max(pi,pR), enthalpy residual by max(cp_i Ti,cp_R TR). A same-sign smooth root minimum would require a sonic tangency: test critical points explicitly. No uncertified unresolved interval may return success.

Report NO_PHYSICAL_BOUNDARY_ROOT for no compatible root in a valid complete interval; BOUNDARY_THERMOCHEMICAL_DOMAIN_VIOLATION for an inadmissible state or truncated necessary wave path; INCOMPATIBLE_INCOMING_STATE for nonunique or causally/entropically incompatible solutions; UNSUPPORTED_SUPERSONIC_INFLOW for a requested external jet; NUMERICAL_BOUNDARY_ROOT_FAILURE for convergence/quadrature failure. Record input stage, intervals, residuals, eigenvalues and wave speeds. Retry only stage-induced failures under NUM-006; invalid configured inputs fail before stepping. Never return zero or extrapolated states as fallback.

## BC-006 — wall and partial opening

Aopen=0 invokes only wall solve; no reservoir EOS query or species injection is needed. Pressure force reaction is recorded separately for the solid; no work at a stationary face. Partial-aperture weighting is the GEN1 compact communicating-face approximation, not a Cd loss. Persistent cells keep their full geometric volume at closure.
