# Area-hybrid increment: prototype, not accepted recipe

The area_hybrid directory is additive research only. It does not edit the reviewed local/global material prototypes, S05 geometry, NASA or the pinned S06 bulk kernel. The cumulative-volume coordinate proposal is a scientific change requiring review/adjudication; it is not described as a refactor.

Endpoint software correction: the complete prior version is preserved under historical/before-endpoint-representation. For accepted A=1+2x², the canonical stored right volume float(5/3) exceeds its exact rational value and the generic frozen inverse rejected initialization. The local AreaState adapter now recognizes only W equal to geometry.cumulative(geometry.segment.right), returns that declared endpoint and records its exact signed volume residual as CANONICAL_RIGHT_ENDPOINT. It does not claim a root bracket for that representational case; no W, energy or inventory is changed. The next representable W above it is rejected, while ordinary interior inverse behavior remains separate. This is an explicit endpoint representation convention confined to this proposal; frozen regional_geometry was not edited.

Eight tests now pass. endpoint_reproducer.py demonstrates historical failure versus corrected initialization. endpoint-fix-equivalence.json confirms all arrays of the previous ten-step rest demonstration remain bitwise identical after the fix. Updated stage records additionally carry endpoint records. This does not expand the scientific acceptance or physical birth/exit scope.

Authoritative AreaState contains face W, extensive inventory I12, labels and the accepted polynomial geometry object. Physical coordinates are derived through the bracketed W inverse, with residuals retained. Thermodynamic recovery operates separately for each represented region. The prototype currently converts a region to the inherited unit-area recovery interface without averaging across regions; this introduces explicit multiply/divide rounding and is not a claim of arithmetic identity for arbitrary area. Ordinary fixed bulk cells use the accepted polynomial's exact area integrals/averages, face areas and the unchanged pinned kernel. Ghosts carry actual regional states, never EOS of an aggregate observation cell.

The local patch uses area-scaled regional flux. Moving material faces return A*p and A*p*u, zero material mass exchange. Ordinary bulk faces use their kernel F multiplied by physical face area. Wdot=A*s shares the stage with I'=Gleft−Gright+pΔA in momentum only. The source is identical in high and low guard candidates, including the positivity-cap construction. Both FE endpoints and the final convex combination are checked. Returned AreaLedger contains separate face integrals and source integrals; consumers must audit both, never mistake this for the old face-only ledger interface.

Reorganization retains material boundaries and authoritative W at existing coordinates. Overlaps use the accepted integral of A, divided by donor ΔW, and the final target receives the exact remaining donor inventory. Source/target order and label checks retain their conservative meaning. The observation projection also integrates volume overlaps; it separately reports physical volume-averaged pressure. No homogeneous EOS is applied to aggregate projection for physical pressure.

Seven initial tests passed: quadratic-area stationary thermal contact with both patch and ordinary bulk cells; explicit A=1 comparison against the frozen hybrid; low-order geometry rejection; inadmissible second-FE EOS rejected despite admissible combination; conservative area-overlap remap; manufactured static-fluid/moving-mesh GCL through both guards; and volume-weighted physical pressure projection. The tested A=1 step matches edges, inventories and integrated face flux bitwise, with zero geometric source. This is one bounded comparison, not automatic transfer of prior numerical PASS.

A 24-cell quadratic-area rest control with N2/600 K and CO2/1800 K, p=100 kPa, runs ten steps at dt=1e-6 s. Maximum pressure deviation is about 1.62e-7 Pa and velocity about 7.62e-11 m/s. The largest unnormalized inventory ledger residual is about 4.73e-13 in energy units. No ST-003 gate is inferred from those raw residuals. Runtime was approximately 2.1 s before source pinning; final logs/results give the exact final run cost and hashes.

The manufactured moving-mesh test prescribes numerical mesh velocity while the physical gas is static. It is an algebra/GCL probe, not an oracle for nonzero-uniform-u flow through variable A. Variable-area physical waves, moving material contacts, convergence sequences and full numerical acceptance remain untested here.

Important open scope: one accepted polynomial segment only; interfaces reaching physical ends, birth/exit inventories, segment joins, full event coupling, and higher-order regional source reconstruction are not implemented. An attempted out-of-domain W fails; no material is deleted or silently transmitted. The current dt predictor is inherited as a preliminary bound and must be reviewed for the volume coordinate before broader use. No general production capability or completion is asserted. Independent s01 review is required before extending this increment.
